FAQ

Common Digital OpportunityQuestions

What is a digital opportunity? +

A digital opportunity is a possibility to use digital technology to solve a business problem,improve an experience,create efficiency,or deliver new value.

How can businesses identify digital opportunities? +

Businesses can identify opportunities by studying customer needs,internal processes,data,business goals,and areas where existing experiences can be improved.

Does digital transformation always require new technology? +

Not always.A business may create significant improvements by simplifying existing processes,improving usability,connecting systems,or using existing technology more effectively.

Why should businesses focus on the problem first? +

Starting with the problem helps businesses avoid adopting technology simply because it is new and instead focus on solutions that create meaningful value.

How does Vertexa help businesses find digital opportunities? +

Vertexa studies business challenges,user needs,processes,technology,and goals to identify practical digital opportunities and develop solutions around them.
