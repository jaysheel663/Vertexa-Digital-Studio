THE BASICS

What AreBusiness Platforms?

Business platforms are digital solutions that help organizations connect people,information,processes and services within one structured environment.

Connecting Business Activities Through Technology

Many businesses depend on different activities such as customer management,communication,sales,operations,reporting and information management.

Without a connected system,these activities may depend on separate tools and manual processes.A business platform can bring these functions together and make information easier to access and manage.

The goal is not simply to create software.The goal is to create a digital environment that supports how a business actually works.

BP

Business Platform

DIGITAL WORKFLOWS

Turning Business ProcessesInto Digital Workflows

Businesses often follow repeated processes every day.These may include receiving requests,reviewing information,approving activities,updating records and generating reports.

A digital platform can represent these processes as structured workflows so that tasks move through the appropriate stages.

A Digital Workflow Can Include

  • Request submission
  • Review and verification
  • Approval processes
  • Data updates
  • Notifications
  • Reporting and tracking

SECURITY

Business Platforms NeedStrong Security

Business platforms may contain important organizational and customer information,making security an important part of digital platform development.

Access Control

Users should only have access to information and functions appropriate for their roles.

Data Protection

Business and customer information should be handled carefully throughout the platform.

Authentication

User identity should be verified before allowing access to protected platform features.

Secure Communication

Secure communication methods help protect information moving between users,applications and servers.

SCALABILITY

Building PlatformsThat Can Grow

A business platform should be designed with the possibility of future growth in mind.

As an organization grows,it may require more users,additional features,larger amounts of data and connections with new systems.

A Scalable Platform Should

  • Support growing numbers of users
  • Handle increasing amounts of information
  • Allow new features to be introduced
  • Support future integrations
  • Maintain reliable performance
