STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

Six Questions Before We Build

01

Where Are We?

Understand the current business,digital presence,customer experience and technology landscape.

02

Who Are We Serving?

Understand audiences,needs,behaviors,expectations and customer journeys.

03

Where Can We Grow?

Identify market opportunities,experience improvements and new digital possibilities.

04

What Should Change?

05

How Will We Build?

Define experience,technology,data,delivery and operating requirements.

06

How Will We Measure?

Create meaningful indicators that connect activity to business outcomes.
